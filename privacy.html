Privacy Policy

Effective September 28, 2026

Renewly does not collect, store or share any personal information. There is no sign-up, no account, and no analytics, advertising or tracking SDK.

Your data

Subscriptions, prices, dates and settings are stored only on your iPhone. They are never uploaded to us or to any third party. The app does not connect to the internet.

Notifications

Payment reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device. No notification content leaves your iPhone.

Backups

If you export a backup, a JSON file is created on your device and shared only where you choose (for example, Files). Importing reads a file you pick.

Data we collect

None.

Children

Renewly does not collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page.
